3. Build

Use Hidden UART0 Bluetooth on pi3b+ as another uart channel, can use 6 steppers this way without motherboard
May need usb to uart adapter.

Used this but wrote own:
https://github.com/kjk25/TMC2209_ESP32/blob/main/tmc/TMC_2209_uart.py

Using pico with UART channels guide for uart channels:

Driver / Motor,MS1 Pin,MS2 Pin,UART Address
Motor 1 (Base),GND (or floating),GND (or floating),0
Motor 2 (Shoulder),High (3.3V),GND,1
Motor 3 (Elbow),GND,High (3.3V),2
Motor 4 (Wrist),High (3.3V),High (3.3V),3

Motor settings at moment
Base - 1500 mA run, 0 mA hold, delay: 200 us
Shoulder - 800 mA run, 200 mA hold, delay: 10us
